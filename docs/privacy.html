Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Data We Collect

JawEase stores your exercise data, pain logs, and preferences locally on your device using AsyncStorage. We do not upload this data to any server.

Email

If you opt in during onboarding, your email address is sent to Resend for our mailing list. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Subscriptions

Subscription purchases are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status but do not store payment details.

Analytics

We may collect anonymous usage analytics (e.g., which screens are visited) to improve the app. No personal health data is shared.
